03 — OPERATING PRINCIPLES

How I approach
the unknown.

The best product decisions are not guesses dressed up as certainty. They are hypotheses tested against evidence, constraints, and outcomes.

01 / OBSERVE

Start with behaviour, not assumptions.

Find what people actually do, where they hesitate, what they abandon, and which workarounds they create. Treat opinions as signals to investigate — not proof.

02 / DIAGNOSE

Understand the cause before choosing a fix.

Separate symptoms from causes. Compare root-cause hypotheses against evidence and resist jumping straight to the most exciting feature idea.

03 / PRIORITISE

Balance user value with business reality.

Evaluate reach, intensity, frequency, strategic fit, feasibility, and expected impact — while considering the needs of the wider ecosystem.

04 / MEASURE

Define success before launch.

Choose a primary outcome, supporting metrics, and guardrails. A shipped feature is an output; a meaningful improvement in user behaviour is the outcome.
