How I think
about products.

Good product work is not about having the most ideas. It’s about building confidence in what matters, then learning quickly from what happens.

01

Start with behaviour, not assumptions.

Look at what people actually do, where they hesitate, what they abandon, and which workarounds they create. Treat opinions as signals to investigate, not proof.

02

Understand the cause before choosing a fix.

Separate the visible symptom from the underlying cause. Compare hypotheses against evidence and avoid jumping straight to a feature because it sounds compelling.

03

Balance user value with business reality.

Prioritise problems using reach, intensity, frequency, strategic fit, feasibility, and expected impact — while considering the needs of the wider ecosystem.

04

Define success before shipping.

Choose a primary outcome, supporting metrics, and guardrails. A shipped feature is an output; a meaningful improvement in user behaviour is the outcome.
